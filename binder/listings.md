# Etsy listings: copy and paste

Four listings, all made from the same pages. More listings means more ways for buyers to find you in Etsy search.

For every listing:

- **Type:** Digital files. **Who made it:** I did. **What is it:** A finished product. **When was it made:** the newest year range in the list.
- **Category:** type `planner` in the category box and pick the closest match (something like *Paper & Party Supplies → Paper → Calendars & Planners*).
- **Photos:** upload `listing/<name>/1-main.jpg`, `2-inside.jpg`, `3-closeup.jpg`, `4-how-it-works.jpg`, in that order. The first one is the thumbnail people see in search.
- **Digital files:** upload both PDFs from `dist/` (US Letter and A4).
- **Quantity:** 999. Digital listings never run out, and Etsy renews them automatically when they sell.
- **Renewal:** Automatic.
