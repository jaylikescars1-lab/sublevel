Privacy

Last updated October 26, 2026

Short version: your garage stays on your phone. SUBLEVEL has no accounts, no servers that hold your data, no analytics and no ads.

What's stored, and where

  • Your cars, build plans, log, money, lists and settings are saved in this browser's storage on your device.
  • Photos you add are saved on your device too.
  • Nothing is uploaded. If you delete the app or clear your browser's website data, it's gone, so export a backup now and then (More → Settings → Export backup).
  • Backup files go wherever you choose to save them.

AI (optional)

AI features are off until you turn them on in Settings, one of two ways:

  • With a SUBLEVEL access code: your request, with the car details it needs to answer, goes to SUBLEVEL's small server, which passes it to Anthropic's API and sends the answer back. The server doesn't store your requests or answers. Your code is saved only on your device.
  • With your own Anthropic API key: the request goes straight from your device to Anthropic. The key is stored only on your device and is never put in backups.

Either way, Anthropic's handling of that data is covered by Anthropic's privacy policy.

Shop links

Shop buttons open a search on Amazon or eBay in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies and may set cookies. SUBLEVEL doesn't see what you search for or buy. Today these are plain searches. If SUBLEVEL joins an affiliate program, it may earn a small commission on purchases at no extra cost to you, and this page will say so.
